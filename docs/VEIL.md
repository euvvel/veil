# Veil

A veil is a thin, semi-transparent covering that both hides and reveals.

It stands between two worlds. On one side is the ordinary, visible realm. On the other side is something concealed — sacred, forbidden, dangerous, or simply unknown. The veil does not destroy the barrier; it *is* the barrier. Yet it is permeable. At the right moment it can be lifted, parted, or seen through, allowing a brief crossing before it falls back into place.

This is why the image appears so often in occult, mystical, and funerary language:

- The veil between the living and the dead
- The veil that hides the face of the divine
- The veil of night, of secrecy, of initiation

It is never a wall. A wall is absolute. A veil is provisional. It promises that something lies just beyond reach — and that, for a moment, the distance can be crossed.

In the context of this project, the name captures exactly that threshold: the brief parting that lets a track pass from the hidden network into your hands, and the inevitable closing that leaves nothing behind.

**Veil** is named for the thin membrane between your world and the Soulseek underworld. It parts just long enough to let a track cross over, then closes again — leaving nothing behind.

## The poem

The name comes from verses Nathaniel Hawthorne wrote in *The Spectator*, an amateur weekly family newspaper he created and edited in his youth:

> Oh could I raise the darken'd veil,
> Which hides my future life from me,
> Could unborn ages slowly sail,
> Before my view—and could I see
> My every action painted there,
> To cast one look I would not dare.
> There poverty and grief might stand,
> And dark Despair's corroding hand,
> Would make me seek the lonely tomb
> To slumber in its endless gloom.
> Then let me never cast a look,
> Within Fate's fix'd mysterious book.

## How the name shapes the project

The metaphor isn't decoration. It is the design rule:

| The veil… | …in veil |
|---|---|
| is provisional, not a wall | The runner exists only while it is needed, then disappears. |
| parts for a brief crossing | A track passes from Soulseek to Telegram, and nothing else does. |
| falls back into place | The file is deleted from the runner after delivery. Only a line of text remains. |
| hides and reveals | The Explore feed shows what lies just beyond what you already know. |
